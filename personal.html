Arcane Fang

In a world scarred by the fall of the ancient Primekin, where ruins pulse with forgotten energy and whispered secrets, Calwyn, a human scholar burdened by an unnatural power, joins forces with Vera, a fierce Beast-kin warrior searching for her missing mother.

Together, they journey through crumbling temples and Aether-infused chambers, uncovering relics of a civilization that once bent reality to its will, and paid the price.

As cultists harness ancient rituals and blood magic to awaken Primekin power, Calwyn and Vera must confront not only their enemies, but the truths buried within themselves. When Calwyn's dormant transformation erupts in a moment of desperation, the lines between man and monster blur. In a race against time and terror, the fate of both their peoples may hinge on what they discover in the heart of the ruins, and how much of themselves they're willing to sacrifice.

Live from Planet Frystar

Galek Zim never wanted to be a mechanic. He especially didn't want to inherit a collapsing repair shop on a frozen wasteland of a planet, or hear a smug, omniscient voice narrating his every misstep.

But when gremlins unionize, customers demand the impossible, and his landlord tries to monetize pest infestations, Galek realizes two things: 1) he might be losing his mind, and 2) that might be the only way to survive this job.

A sci-fi satire about bad decisions, reluctant heroism, and the unbearable weight of being narratable.
